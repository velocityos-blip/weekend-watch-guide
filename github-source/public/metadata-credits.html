Stories deserve their credits.

Our recommendations, short descriptions, and mood tags are editorial. Additional metadata is matched by exact title and release year. Missing information is left unavailable.

Movie metadata

Cast and source genres come from the Wikipedia movie dataset, released under the MIT license by Peter Rust. It covers American movies through 2023 and is archived; it is not a current release feed. Each enriched title links to its Wikipedia article. No Wikipedia synopsis text or poster images are reproduced.

Series metadata

Cast, series status, community ratings and poster links come from TVmaze. Adapted series metadata is available under CC BY-SA 4.0. Changes: fields selected, cast shortened and records matched to our curated IDs. The metadata is available in the public catalog JSON under each title’s metadata field. Original images belong to their respective owners.

Data is cached; the title details show when it was retrieved. Community ratings are not predictions of your personal taste. Streaming availability is not supplied by these sources. This app is not affiliated with IMDb, Wikipedia or TVmaze.
