# Growth experiment: search discovery

Status: source changes prepared on 19 November 2026. Hostinger deployment, Search Console verification, and website AdSense approval are not completed.

## First experiment
Use the existing Weekend Watch Guide domain and hosting. Ship three focused evergreen watch guides, linked from the homepage, plus robots.txt and an XML sitemap. No claim of keyword volume, indexing, rankings, or current streaming availability has been verified. These topics are test hypotheses chosen from the existing catalog.

## Sequence after deployment access is available
1. Confirm that the live release serves the new guide pages, CSS, robots.txt and sitemap.xml with correct status codes and production canonicals.
2. Verify domain ownership in Search Console and submit the sitemap once. Record indexing status and the baseline performance report.
3. Resolve the existing launch work in RUNNING.md: persistent storage/backups, operator contact, accurate privacy/retention information and account recovery/deletion.
4. Confirm the existing AdSense account can accept websites. Submit only when the site is ready and any required agreement is approved by the owner. Do not assume approval or insert ads before eligibility is established.
5. Measure search impressions, clicks, landing-page visits and discovery-tool usage. Hosting visitors alone are not verified organic traffic.
6. Expand only topics with evidence of demand. Add original useful selection notes, not bulk duplicate pages.
7. Reassess after four weeks of live data, allowing for crawl/indexing delay. Decide to improve, continue or pause based on traffic quality and cost.

## Economics
Track actual receipts minus hosting, domain renewals, APIs, subscriptions and promotion. Allocate shared costs explicitly. Revenue and profit are currently unverified for this experiment; the previous AdSense dashboard showed zero this month. Avoid additional purchases until there is a concrete test and a spending limit.

Advertising is the initial monetization hypothesis. Paid products, affiliate links, sponsorships or selling the site require separate validation of demand and account/program eligibility. A site sale should be assessed from actual traffic and earnings rather than registrar valuation estimates.

## Weekly scorecard
- Search Console indexed guide URLs, impressions, clicks, CTR and queries.
- Landing-page visits and discovery-tool engagement, excluding owner/test activity where feasible.
- Actual monetization status and receipts.
- Costs, net profit, and the next change supported by evidence.

No background publishing, monitoring or recurring work has been scheduled.
